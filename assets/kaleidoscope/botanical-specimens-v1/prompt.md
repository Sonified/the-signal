# Set 5 — Botanical specimens

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool in a photographic style; not actual photographs. Packed into botanical-specimens-128.png and wired in as Image set 5 (see README.md).

## Prompt

Botanical specimen photography. A collection of real leaves, individual flower petals, and small flowers photographed from directly overhead, then carefully cut out onto transparency. They should look like ordinary fresh specimens collected from a garden and photographed with a good camera in soft daylight. Accurate natural colors, visible leaf veins, delicate petal texture, slightly curled edges, small natural imperfections. A mix of different green leaves, fern pieces, a few autumn leaves, loose petals, and simple small flowers. No illustration, rendering, decorative embellishment, glow, or invented plants.

Delivery format only: square transparent PNG contact sheet, exactly 8 columns and 8 rows, 64 separate specimens. One complete specimen centered inside each equal square cell with generous clear space around it. All specimens fully visible, no overlap or cropped edges. Genuine alpha transparency; no paper, surface, scene, labels, text, or grid lines.
