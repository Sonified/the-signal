## Prompt

Preserve the reference petals' realistic photographic appearance, natural colors, delicate veins, and fine texture. Replace 32 cells with single fresh green leaves, interspersed evenly among 32 petals. Keep the camera directly overhead for every specimen. Every leaf lies flat with its broad face fully visible, tip pointing up and base pointing down; petals also have their base down. Consistent straight-on orientation throughout, no side views, dramatic angles, twisting, or foreshortening. One detached petal or one leaf per cell. No whole flowers, branches, bouquets, illustrations, or added embellishment.

Preserve the square 8-column by 8-row atlas layout: exactly 64 isolated specimens, centered in equal square cells, fully contained with generous transparent padding. Genuine alpha transparency. No surface, background scene, text, labels, or grid lines.
